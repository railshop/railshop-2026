/// SPATIAL MARK SPECIFICATIONS

The horizontal aspect ratio of the primary logo fundamentally breaks inside the 1:1 mathematical constraints of Favicons and standard Social Media UI bounds (Instagram, LinkedIn). To solve this, the text is compressed into a singular brutalist monogram (`R.`) engineered to fill pure geometric space while retaining the agency's primary typography and punctuation sign-off.

03. Color Matrix

Electric Primary

HEX: #38b6ff

Used explicitly for interaction drivers, logo punctuation, and tactical hover states. Do not over-saturate layouts with this color.

Void Dark (Base)

HEX: #050505

The foundational canvas replacing pure black. Implements a slight offset to prevent OLED smearing while creating extreme contrast.

Structure Mid

HEX: #111111

Used internally to raise grid elements, feature cards, and structural architecture above the Void Dark baseline.

Typography Fill

HEX: #e2e8f0

Replaces pure white for body copy to eliminate eye strain. Typically deployed at 60% opacity for subtext, and pure white for massive H1/H2s.

05. Structural Styling

Rigid Geometry

Absolutely zero `border-radius` application. Every component strictly inherits a razor-sharp right angle structurally reflecting engineering schematics.

Grid Boundaries

Components operate inside overlapping structural grid lines generated via `border-white/10`, representing continuous, scalable blueprints.

Hover Diagnostics

Links and tactical arrays implement hyper-fast CSS transformations. Electric Blue overrides borders and specific prefixes upon cursor interaction.
