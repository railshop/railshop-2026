## The Strategy
Generating leads is only half the battle. If a prospect fills out a form at 11:00 PM and nobody replies until 9:00 AM the next day, that lead is already calling another contractor. Speed to lead is the single most critical factor in securing modern service contracts.

## The Solution
We build practical, automated workflows directly into industry systems like ServiceTitan, Jobber, or GoHighLevel. Whether it's setting up automated missed-call text backs, intelligent chat responses for after-hours scheduling, or streamlining back-office paperwork, we help you fundamentally eliminate the manual bottleneck in your business.
