### The Baseline
Country Farms operates a highly successful, multi-faceted landscaping, hardscaping, and garden center operation out of Greensburg, PA. While their physical footprint was heavily established, maintaining a consistent brand and capturing the right kind of leads across so many different service categories presented a unique challenge.

### The Architecture
To simplify things, we stepped in to manage their entire digital marketing footprint. We established a cohesive online presence, starting with a thoughtful, conversion-focused <a href="https://www.countryfarmsinc.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">website</a> that clearly organized their individual service divisions. We backed this up with beautiful, active-job photography to ensure their online brand matched the premium quality of their real-world installations.

### The Deployment
To help them stand out regionally, we deployed a comprehensive, multi-channel approach. We utilized highly visual Meta ads to showcase gorgeous patio and hardscape builds, while using Google Local Service Ads to connect with homeowners actively searching for high-intent services. All of this was supported by a practical, localized SEO campaign.

### The Yield
This unified, ongoing strategy completely stabilized their lead flow and established a reliable local footprint. We helped secure consistent top placements in the Google Map Pack across all of their primary service areas. The operation now enjoys a 100% year-over-year increase in qualified web traffic, bringing in high-value landscaping and construction leads at a very efficient cost-per-acquisition of under $40.
