### The Baseline
Cedar Creek Design focuses on landscape and hardscape architecture, specializing in beautiful, high-end outdoor structures. Because they focus on larger, premium residential projects, their digital presence needed to reflect that level of quality. Standard contact forms were bringing in too many out-of-scope inquiries. They needed a modernized website capable of gently filtering leads so they could focus their time on the right clientele.

### The Architecture
We built a completely custom, high-performance <a href="https://www.cedarcreekla.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">website</a> on Webflow. The aesthetic was designed to naturally showcase the detail and craftsmanship of their architectural installations. Under the hood, we developed a clean, user-friendly multi-step quote request sequence. This mechanism helps guide incoming visitors to thoughtfully share their budget and project scope, ensuring both the client and the team know if it's a good fit before hopping on a call.

### The Deployment
To help the right people find their new site, we executed a clear, two-pronged strategy. We implemented a solid local SEO campaign to help them rank naturally for structural hardscaping searches in their area. Alongside that, we set up targeted Google Local Service Ads to connect with homeowners actively looking for premium contractors.

### The Yield
The new system stabilized their lead pipeline almost immediately. The Webflow site and SEO efforts combined to drive a 175% increase in highly-qualified web traffic. Thanks to the multi-step intake flow, the quality of conversations their team is having has vastly improved. Over on the paid side, the Local Service Ads are running incredibly well, consistently bringing in top-tier leads at a very healthy $36 Cost Per Acquisition.
