### The Baseline
Fire & Ice Heating & Air operates in a highly competitive Pennsylvania market. While their field service team was top-notch, their digital marketing felt a bit disconnected prior to 2020. Without clear tracking for their lead generation, it was incredibly difficult for their team to confidently pace their advertising spend or accurately measure return on investment.

### The Architecture
We built a unified, friendly digital presence anchored by a custom Webflow <a href="https://www.fireandicepros.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">website</a>. Rather than relying on scattered data points, we worked to map their entire communication flow directly into ServiceTitan. This helpful shift finally provided complete visibility—from the initial Google click all the way to the generated invoice.

### The Deployment
With clear analytics in place, we set up highly targeted Google Search and Local Service Ads (LSA). By running these practical paid campaigns alongside a steady local SEO strategy, we were able to deliver a reliable, compounding 150% year-over-year increase in qualified organic web traffic.

### The Yield
By making clear, data-driven decisions rather than relying on guesswork, we helped scale the operation's total revenue by over 4X. Today, their campaigns consistently generate a very healthy 8X return on ad spend (ROAS), keeping their average cost-per-acquisition (CPA) on local service leads safely below $50.
